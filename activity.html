Together for Better Understanding our Sexual & Reproductive Health and Rights

◷ 22 August 2024 ⚐ Domiz 2 Syrian Refugee Camp ♀ Women & girls, ages 15–49

Under the title "Together for Better Understanding our Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights," Nujeen held a series of awareness sessions on family planning for women and girls aged 15 to 49.

The sessions took place inside the Domiz 2 Syrian refugee camp, where Nujeen has maintained an ongoing presence supporting displaced families. Working directly within displacement venues allows the organization to reach women who would otherwise have limited access to reproductive health education and services.

What the sessions covered

Each session combined practical family planning guidance with open discussion designed to ease the social stigma that often surrounds reproductive health. Facilitators worked in small groups so that every participant felt comfortable asking questions.

  • Family planning methods and informed choice
  • Maternal and reproductive health fundamentals
  • A rights-based understanding of reproductive health
  • Clear referral pathways to local health services

Over the course of the program, participants moved from hesitation toward genuine engagement — asking questions, sharing experiences, and carrying what they learned back to their families. That shift, more than any single statistic, is what the work is for.
