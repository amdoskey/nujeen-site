What we do

Five fields, one commitment to family wellbeing.

Each strand of our work reinforces the others — education leads to access, access builds trust, and trust opens the door to lasting change.

01

Reproductive Health Education

Community outreach and awareness series on sexual and reproductive health and rights, delivered in plain language and safe spaces.

→
02

Family Planning

Targeted sessions for women and youth, focused on girls and women aged 11 to 49, grounded in informed choice.

→
03

Refugee & IDP Support

Direct operations and education inside displacement venues, including a continuous presence at the Domiz 2 camp.

→
04

Legal & Policy Advocacy

Working alongside academic institutions and public health bodies to shape healthcare and legal reform.

→
05

Youth Empowerment

Partnering with IFMSA Kurdistan on student health festivals that break stigma and open honest conversation.
