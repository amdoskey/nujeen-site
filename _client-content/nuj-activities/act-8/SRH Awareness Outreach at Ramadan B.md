**SRH Awareness Outreach at Ramadan Bazar**



Nujeen for Family Democratizing Organization has sponsored and participated with SCORA-IFMSA KURDISTAN, in a community Outreach activity on raising awareness on different topics of SRH that was conducted by the students of Medicine collage, University of Duhok in Ramadan Bazar at Duhok Mall.

This activity included providing different IEC materials as well as providing consultations from our professional staff.
